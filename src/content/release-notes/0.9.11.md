## Updates keep your permissions

- **No more re-allowing after updates (Mac).** Voxflow is now signed the same way every release, so macOS remembers your Microphone and Accessibility permissions when you update.
- **One last time:** the update to this version may get stuck on the Accessibility screen. Quit Voxflow, run `tccutil reset Accessibility com.voxflow.app` in Terminal, reopen it and click **Allow**. After that, updates won't ask again.
