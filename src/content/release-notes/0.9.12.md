## Meeting mode (Mac, beta)

- **Record a meeting.** The new **Meetings** page records your microphone and the sound playing on your Mac, so everyone on a Zoom, Meet or Teams call is included. Press **Stop and save** and the meeting is transcribed on your computer and added to History. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Allow it once.** The first time, macOS asks to let Voxflow record system audio. Without it, only your own voice is recorded.
- **From a script.** `voxflow --toggle-meeting` starts and stops a meeting from Terminal or a shortcut app.
