## Corrections and links

- **Fix yourself mid-sentence.** Say "scratch that" (or "never mind", "make that", "no wait") and then the right words. "Let's eat McDonald's tomorrow, scratch that, Burger King" types "Let's eat Burger King tomorrow". Only the last few words are replaced, the live preview follows along, and History keeps what you originally said.
- **Add a link by voice.** Copy a link, then say "check out this link" and Voxflow adds it after the words. Say "paste link" to type any text you copied. Both are on the **Snippets** page.
- **Ignore background voices.** A new option in General, under Sound, skips speech that is much quieter than yours.
- **Dictionary, Styles and stats.** A Dictionary page, per-app writing styles, and words-per-week stats on the History page.
