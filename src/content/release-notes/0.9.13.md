## Fixes

- **No more stray periods.** A short noise or silent clip that comes back as just "." or "?" is no longer pasted.
- **Post-processing can't hang dictation.** If the AI cleanup doesn't answer within 60 seconds, your original text is pasted.
- **More audio files.** Transcribe voice notes in Opus formats (ogg, opus, webm).
- **Clearer messages.** A muted or silent microphone, and a missing model, now say so instead of failing quietly.
- **Self-healing transcription.** After a GPU error, transcription restarts on its own and falls back to the CPU if needed.
- **Stuck on Accessibility (Mac).** If macOS won't let you allow Accessibility after an update, the permissions screen now shows the one-line Terminal command that fixes it, with a Copy button.
- **Live bars follow your voice.** The recording bars now show your voice as a wave: flat dots when you pause, rippling outward when you talk, even on a quiet microphone.
- **Last words show up.** The live preview now shows your last few words right after you pause, instead of waiting for new speech.
- **Faster first start.** The microphone opens when Voxflow launches, so the first dictation no longer waits for it.
