## Fixes

- **No more stray periods.** A short noise or silent clip that comes back as just "." or "?" is no longer pasted.
- **Post-processing can't hang dictation.** If the AI cleanup doesn't answer within 60 seconds, your original text is pasted.
- **More audio files.** Transcribe voice notes in Opus formats (ogg, opus, webm).
- **Clearer messages.** A muted or silent microphone, and a missing model, now say so instead of failing quietly.
- **Self-healing transcription.** After a GPU error, transcription restarts on its own and falls back to the CPU if needed.
