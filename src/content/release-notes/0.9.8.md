## Welcome to Voxflow

Voxflow is a free, open source voice dictation app that runs entirely on your computer. It's built on the Voxflow engine, with new features on top.

- **Snippets** — say a trigger phrase like "my address" and Voxflow types the saved text. Find them in **Snippets** in the sidebar.
- **Accuracy and speed** — the model picker now shows each model's accuracy grade (A is best) and speed.
- **New look** — Voxflow's own logo, colors and font, with clearer buttons and switches.
