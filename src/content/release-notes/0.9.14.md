## Fixes

- **Safer API keys.** Keys for AI providers are now kept in your system's credential store instead of a settings file.
- **Local AI cleanup works.** Post-processing with a provider on your own computer (like Ollama or LM Studio) connects directly again.
- **Linux paste in terminals.** Shift+Insert now pastes in terminals and is the default on Linux.
- **Tray icon remembers its spot.** Desktops now keep your tray icon settings between launches.
- **No more custom words read back.** A transcript that is only your custom word list is dropped instead of pasted.
- **Overlay stays out of the way.** The recording overlay no longer steals focus on Linux X11.
- **New languages.** Armenian, Hungarian and Thai translations.
- **Windows warning.** Voxflow now warns you if it is running as administrator, where pasting into normal windows fails.
